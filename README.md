## What This Is

GUES is a structured theoretical conjecture proposing that spacetime geometry is not a fundamental substrate but an emergent property of information flow — and that this reframing has consequences for how we understand the fundamental forces, black holes, and the observed acceleration of cosmic expansion.

This is not a proven theory. It is not a completed framework. It is an honest E1 conjecture: a falsifiable claim with explicit connections to established physics, proposed mechanisms, and open questions clearly labeled as such.

The acronym is intentional. This is an educated guess. We know what it is.

-----

## The Central Claim

Treating information flow as a given substrate, spacetime geometry emerges from its structure rather than being fundamental — a distinction that would manifest as measurable deviations from smooth manifold behavior at information-dense boundaries.

The fundamental forces correspond to distinct symmetry configurations within that emergent geometry, such that their unification requires identifying the higher-dimensional geometric object those symmetry groups are faces of.

Black holes function as information-to-geometry transducers whose cumulative geometric output is a candidate mechanism for observed cosmic expansion, distinguishable from vacuum energy by its correlation with black hole density distribution.

## What This Is Not

- A claim about the source or origin of information — information flow is taken as given
- A completed mathematical framework — the formalism is in development
- A challenge to General Relativity or the Standard Model — this conjecture operates at a deeper level and must reproduce their results
- Proven — nothing here has passed Gate 3 or above under the Charter
