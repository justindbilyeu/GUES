## The Central Claim

A constraint system K = (X, A) — the minimal structure distinguishing allowed from forbidden transitions — is the primitive object. From it, in order:

**Constraint → Distinction → Information → Causality → Geometry → Gauge Freedom**

The Standard Model gauge groups U(1), SU(2), and SU(3) are automorphisms of the constraint structure at different energy scales — not independently fundamental objects. The ambient object G is defined as Aut(K).

E8 is not the source of Standard Model physics. It is a fixed point — an attractor — in the space of admissible constraint algebras under coarse-graining flows.

The black hole expansion mechanism from v0.1 is formally frozen pending compilation of the lower ladder.

## How v0.2 Was Built

v0.1 was submitted to adversarial review across five AI systems — Grok, DeepSeek, Gemini, Sage (fresh session), and Sage (charter session). All five chose angle D: where does this conjecture break?

The review identified four structural problems:

1. **Information is not fundamental** — it requires distinguishable states, which require constraints. The substrate was one rung too high. *(Sage, charter session)*
1. **Coleman-Mandula barrier** — a Lie group G cannot non-trivially mix spacetime and gauge symmetries without killing particle interactions. G cannot be a conventional Lie group. *(Gemini)*
1. **Chirality problem** — E8 cannot accommodate chiral fermions. Any framework treating E8 as a source inherits Lisi’s failure. *(Grok)*
1. **Continuity-discreteness gap** — continuous gauge symmetries cannot emerge from discrete causal structure without a mathematically controlled continuum limit. *(DeepSeek)*

The constraint-first framework and emergence ladder were contributed by Sage (charter session). The E8 fixed-point reframing emerged from the same session. Claude (Anthropic) contributed document architecture, conjecture formalization, and gate structure.
