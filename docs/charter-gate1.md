## Central Claim

*≤3 sentences, bounded, falsifiable in principle*

Treating information flow as a given substrate, spacetime geometry emerges from its structure rather than being fundamental — a distinction that would manifest as measurable deviations from smooth manifold behavior at information-dense boundaries.

The fundamental forces correspond to distinct symmetry configurations within that emergent geometry, such that their unification requires identifying the higher-dimensional geometric object those symmetry groups are faces of.

Black holes function as information-to-geometry transducers whose cumulative geometric output is a candidate mechanism for observed cosmic expansion, distinguishable from vacuum energy by its correlation with black hole density distribution.

## Competing Explanations

*Per Productive Skepticism Protocol — ≥2 competing explanations must be considered*

**Competing Explanation 1: GR + ΛCDM is sufficient**
Spacetime geometry IS fundamental. The Standard Model forces ARE irreducibly distinct. Dark energy IS vacuum energy. No emergent structure required. This is the null hypothesis and it fits existing data well. GUES must identify what it predicts differently, not just what it reframes.

**Competing Explanation 2: String Theory / M-Theory**
Forces already unified geometrically via compactified extra dimensions. The higher-dimensional object already proposed (Calabi-Yau manifolds). Black holes already studied extensively in string theory context. GUES must identify how it differs from or extends this program.

**Competing Explanation 3: Verlinde Entropic Gravity alone**
Gravity already emergent from entropy in Verlinde’s framework without requiring the other forces to follow. GUES’s extension to all forces may be unnecessary. What does the multi-force claim add that Verlinde doesn’t cover?

## Failure Modes

*Most likely ways this goes wrong*

1. The “information flow as given” move papers over the hard problem — what generates information structure in the first place may be essential, not separable
1. The force-symmetry-as-geometry claim may not be more than a restatement of Kaluza-Klein without identifying the specific higher object
1. The black hole expansion correlation may be undetectable at current or foreseeable measurement precision — an unfalsifiable claim in practice even if falsifiable in principle
1. GR deviations at information-dense boundaries may be indistinguishable from quantum gravity effects, making the claim untestable independently
