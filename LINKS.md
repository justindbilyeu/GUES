# GUES — Related Repositories and Intellectual Lineage

## Direct Dependencies

### [The-Charter](https://github.com/justindbilyeu/The-Charter)

**Role:** Governing methodology  
**Relationship:** All work in this repository is conducted under the Research Assistant Charter. The Charter’s gates, evidence hierarchy, and productive skepticism protocol are the quality control layer for every claim made here. GUES does not validate the Charter — the Charter governs GUES.

-----

### [Resonance_Geometry](https://github.com/justindbilyeu/Resonance_Geometry)

**Role:** Rigorous dynamical systems work  
**Relationship:** The sibling project. Where GUES is a cosmological conjecture, Resonance_Geometry is working experimental and simulation-based science — the RFO Stability Wedge, Toy Universe, and Non-Hopf transitions. The two projects share a root intuition about geometry as a dynamic, learning structure, but operate at different scales and evidence levels. Results from Resonance_Geometry that bear on GUES claims will be explicitly cited.

-----

### [ResonanceGeometry](https://github.com/justindbilyeu/ResonanceGeometry)

**Role:** Philosophical and experiential origin  
**Relationship:** The upstream repo. The intuitions that generated GUES — geometry as emergent, information as substrate, forces as shapes in a shared space — lived here first, in less structured form. GUES is what happens when those intuitions are run through Charter discipline. ResonanceGeometry remains the home for the philosophical layer this work deliberately sets aside: the source of information, consciousness as projection, emotion as curvature. Those questions are real. They just belong in a different document.

## What GUES Is Not Claiming to Replace

GUES is a conjecture operating at a level beneath General Relativity and the Standard Model. It must reproduce their results. It is not in competition with them — it is asking what they emerge from.

Any result from GR or the Standard Model that appears to conflict with GUES is a constraint on GUES, not evidence against GR.
